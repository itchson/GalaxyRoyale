# Supplied WebGPU capture inspection

I opened all five Root-supplied images using view_image at original resolution. Root performed the browser actions and identifies these as normal-build `dacaf7a` WebGPU captures. This is an independent still-image inspection, not my own browser/GPU execution, runtime identity verification, performance measurement or user art approval. The source reviews remain separate and unchanged. All five filenames end.png but their actual bytes are JFIF JPEG; the pinning format guard caught that before copying. Exact bytes/names are preserved and image/jpeg is recorded. Lossy encoding excludes pixel-exact color/edge conclusions.

At1280×720, planet-webgpu shows a legible spherical body: curved surface bands, a brighter upper-left hemisphere and darker right edge, with a restrained ring around the silhouette. The planet is visually separate from the sharp green RAICHO and its hull marks. The image supports the intended spherical material direction; it does not prove movement, collisions, every biome palette or reduced-detail parity.

Galaxy-webgpu shows a faint but coherent inclined disk with a warm central population and scattered interior light. The former whole-width leaf-like gap and detached traced arms are absent from this view. The dark background and sparse nearby stars leave negative space; the detailed craft and cratered asteroid silhouettes remain distinct. Spiral detail is subtle rather than a bright diagram. This is one seed/view/time and does not prove visibility on every display or contrast during crowded combat.

Nebula-webgpu shows unequal purple billows with separated dark pockets and an intervening bridge. The wispy hard filament remains visible within the cloud. Its contrast is lower than the foreground craft and two nearby rocks; neither is hidden by the captured gas. The still supports the new cloud outline, not fluid physics, animated smoothness, emitted-light behavior or all camera/quality transitions.

Settings-webgpu at1280×720 has a quiet neutral charcoal backing, fine platinum trim and a small upper header wing. SETTINGS/Close, both section headings, all five audio rows, display labels and toggles, Fullscreen, Session/Save and the device footer are readable and visibly separated. No text/control/Close overlap is apparent in this frame. The neutral fill does not visibly inherit the old green tint. The large Close icon remains recognizable; decorative detail stays subordinate to the controls.

Settings-webgpu-848 at848×480 retains readable two-column labels, slider tracks and toggle boxes with a clear Close region. The visible labels do not spill into adjacent controls or the frame, and the footer is separated from the last visible Voice row. Session/Save is below the current visible content region. This still alone cannot prove its scroll reachability, focus behavior or pointer hit geometry; it is not evidence of an unreachable route. Root's actual scroll/input checks and any later lower-scroll capture are the relevant acceptance gate.
