## Evidence preservation and limits

Browser files are actual JPEG bytes with `.jpg` extensions. Native contact
images are offline software projections of actual FrameBuilder packets, not
GPU screenshots. `engine/native-evidence.zip` preserves all85 manifest-pinned
raw files, including helper raster code, baseline/preliminary/final packets
and logs under their original `work/` paths. Its enclosed manifest pins exact
source commits and bytes. This keeps repeated packet coordinates compressed.
The final contact, handoff and manifest are also directly readable beside it.

User style approval, moving/crowded combat perception, natural campaign
completion, cold/warm latency, frame-time/RSS, physical-phone testing and the
30-minute soak remain separate gates. Audio and original theme recording are
unchanged in this pass. No astronomical simulation, performance improvement,
full-game visual perfection or universal browser compatibility is claimed.
