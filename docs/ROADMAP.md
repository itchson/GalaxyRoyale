# Accepted future scope

Mission Control is the authoritative task/status record. This document preserves the latest direction relayed by the Producer from the user's feedback; it does not mark future features implemented or tested. The current playable build remains a solo local-progression slice.

## Owned docks and arenas

The solo champion campaign is now implemented: eight seeded NPC arenas, distinct pixel hulls and attacks, single-claim patches/blueprints, Haven crafting/switching, and a nine-entrant round robin. The eight player duels run live; the other 28 pairings are explicitly simulated. An actual first-place result grants the Sovereign and credits once; completed losses permit another season. Per-ship XP, level-gated purchases, bounded material cargo, death reports, and 0.6–1.6× world zoom are implemented. Native and component tests cover their rules; a complete eight-champion browser playthrough, balance review and physical-device validation remain pending. See CAMPAIGN.md and NARRATIVE.md. Optional local text/voice providers are injection contracts with safe authored fallback; no neural dialogue or voice service is shipped or claimed as running.

Future player-owned docks will support upgrades to their protection radius and arena encounters. Protection must use the same radius for enemy spawning, combat interaction, visuals and player eligibility. Upgrade costs, radius bounds, arena entry/exit rules and rewards still need design approval and play testing. Persistent ownership and upgrades need migration and single-claim reward checks. Multiplayer dock ownership must be enforced by the authoritative session rather than accepted from a client save.

## Co-op and network authority

The accepted co-op direction is eight participants including the host. The central dock exposes Invite and Join; Invite creates an encrypted hexadecimal invitation, and Join resolves an available host session. Every guest uses the host's world seed and protected home dock. When the host quits or joins another world, the session ends and guests return to their own saved local worlds. Host migration is outside this milestone.

WebRTC carries game traffic in a host-to-guest star. A small signaling service resolves invitations and negotiates connections; real cross-network acceptance additionally requires measured ICE/TURN configuration. Signaling and admission are under development. They do not yet implement shared gameplay. The Rust authority milestone must replace the current single-player assumptions with participant-owned input, bodies, projectile ownership, targeting, damage, rewards and local-save boundaries. Remote world state must never overwrite a participant's local world seed or identity. Pending participants count toward the eight-person limit. Version mismatch, expired codes, failed connections and host departure require explicit recovery tests.

Acceptance requires actual simultaneous clients sharing movement, destructible cells, missions and single-claim rewards, followed by host departure and safe return to each local world. Reconnect and compatible progression transfer need their own tested rules. No playable co-op session, public signaling service or TURN deployment is accepted in the current slice. This authority milestone is also a prerequisite for multiplayer battle royale.

## Separate battle royale mode

The future multiplayer mode uses a compact seeded circular universe with equally spaced zones, arena siege play and a last-player-standing outcome. Match state and rewards must remain separate from solo exploration progression. The accepted direction does not yet specify player counts, zone contents, siege damage rules, elimination/respawn rules, match length or matchmaking; these remain design and balance work.

Crossing the circular boundary must transfer the ship to the antipodal edge, preserve its heading and speed, and rebase the camera so smooth following does not sweep across the universe. The portable, opt-in `CircularTopology` primitive in `crates/engine_core/src/topology.rs` is implemented. Native tests verify overshoot and repeated/oblique crossings, explicit invalid/tangent handling, and shared rebase of motion, heading, camera and destination. It is unused by the solo game. Integrating this primitive with a seeded zone layout, interpolation, attached effects, nearby projectiles and authoritative multiplayer state remains future work requiring visible play review.

## Acceptance remains explicit

Desktop exploration now has a live ship-status view on Escape, separate map/settings icons, and a discovery-only universe chart with real seeded refuge geometry and course selection. Holding left continually thrusts along the ship's facing; holding right steers the firing direction. Releasing left restores arrival braking. Coil bodies attach through occupied overlapping joints during both simulation and interpolated rendering. These rules have native and browser-shell regression evidence; real held-mouse timing and player balance still need human play review.

Home and eligible remote refuge belts have seeded orbital motion. Their physical sanctuary shields reflect asteroids at the body-expanded radius, including fast swept contacts, redirected paths and post-collision penetration correction. Home rocks retain basic T1 drops and no XP; remote materials retain their canonical birth tier. Shield contact produces no rewards or damage. Native tests include actual projectile knockback followed by game-tick reflection. Remote tier proof uses an explicitly controlled reposition and does not claim naturally earned traversal.

The current progression model banks carried crafting materials only on physical Haven return. Cargo is lost on death, restart, title exit or reconstruction; stored materials, points, owned compatible abilities and individual ship XP remain. Ships have one offensive, defensive and passive slot, and ranks are capped by that ship's level. Soft fractional particle quads, emissive trails and layered cosmic gas use the shared renderer; desktop captures and CPU measurements do not establish physical-phone quality or frame-rate acceptance.

Browser/WASM updates now use content-addressed complete binding sets and each service worker's own cached shell. The actual earlier Chrome profile exposed the old stable-WASM mismatch; that observed failure and subsequent upgrade checks are retained separately from clean-start observations.

The subsequent presentation milestone uses one native bottom strip, ship-following vital tracks and shared control/boss reservations instead of repeated framed flight modules. Real semantic hit geometry is distinct from the compact glyph slot; measured collisions hide unsafe contact hits. The authored score reads real sanctuary, biome, champion visits/duels, pursuit, hull and exhaust state. Bounded musical transitions, low-hull alarm, thrust voices and effect cues have native/mix/recorded-graph tests; those tests do not establish audible quality or device DSP. Short desktop composition/recovery, human listening and physical-phone acceptance are recorded separately for the exact package.

All fifteen ordinary family bosses and five-generation promotion are implemented. Higher-generation fights keep credit until actual defeat; legacy aggregate counters migrate into generation one without rewards. Signature abilities evolve with their family defeats, and the victorious Sovereign has intrinsic fifth-generation signatures. Independent native projectile fights defeated all fifteen and verified phase coverage, diagnostic isolation and naturally queued G5 retreat/reload/death/retry. Automated IndexedDB and both-WASM snapshot proofs passed. Full natural progression, human balance and physical-device acceptance remain pending; see [QA_BOSSES.md](QA_BOSSES.md).

The top black drawer slides over 180 ms, retains pause/input capture until closure and supports reduced motion. The Producer recorded desktop browser acceptance for console/RoyaleMono/material lighting, compact HUD, WebGPU/WebGL2 startup, synthetic initializer fallback and actual device-loss recovery retaining expedition state.
