# Kikai license and corporation canon

KIKAI are technological life descended from humans in a future where biological life can no longer survive. They are people with friendships, ambitions and hobbies. DORA is the female chassis designation with long hair, DORO male with short spiky hair, and TALO neutral with a crew cut. Player identities have a main manufacturer, a sub-manufacturer, and a cryptographically generated hexadecimal license hash. Hair shape follows the droid type; hair color follows the main corporation. Type-specific player portraits remain a separate art task; recoloring DORA does not produce a DORO or TALO portrait.

The full format is `TYPE-MAIN-SUB-HASH`. Lore shorthand takes the O/A/T type prefix, main initial and sub-manufacturer initial followed by `#HASH`. The guide is **DORA-PAN-RENKO-1337**, called **OPR#1337**. Female callsigns start with O, male with A, and neutral with T; the next initials identify main and then sub-manufacturer. Her green hair identifies PAN Dynamics; her red crystal horns identify RENKO Cybernetics as sub-manufacturer. The user's latest clarification on 11 October 2026 supersedes the earlier ORP name and reversed colors. RENKO retains its confirmed spelling.

## Galaxy Royale and sponsorship

Galaxy Royale is the universe's largest and most popular sport. Arena champions compete for recognition, and manufacturer corporations sponsor arenas and competitors. A sponsor is distinct from a KIKAI's main and sub-manufacturers: sponsorship does not recolor hair or horns, change a license, or grant a ship ability.

The user's example **APP#F344FEE** is a male DORO-PAN-PAN champion representing PAN in a PAN-sponsored arena. **ORP#2323** is a female DORA-RENKO-PAN competitor who could also be sponsored by PAN. These are authored roster candidates; the existing eight encounters must not silently be renamed or have their progression reset. Arena presentation should identify sponsor, champion identity, patch and blueprint separately. Discovery gates still hide unknown opponents and rewards.
